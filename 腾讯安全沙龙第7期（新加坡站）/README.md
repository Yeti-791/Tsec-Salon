# AAAI 2026 Singapore · Towards Trustworthy and Controllable Next-Generation AI Agents

> 发布者：云鼎实验室（Tencent Yunding Lab）　|　Published: January 22, 2026　|　Guangdong, China

> When AI evolves from "conversation" to "action," how can we ensure it is safe, reliable, and trustworthy?

2026年1月27日，**AAAI 2026 Workshop《How Can We Trust and Control Agentic AI?》** 已于新加坡博览中心（Singapore EXPO）成功举办。本次研讨会由腾讯云安全（Tencent Cloud Security）与 **A\*STAR（新加坡科技研究局，Agency for Science, Technology and Research）** 等团队联合举办，汇聚了全球顶尖研究力量，深入探讨了智能体的可信可控问题，携手为 Agentic AI 技术奠定了坚实的安全基石。

> On January 27, 2026, the AAAI 2026 Workshop "How Can We Trust and Control Agentic AI?" was successfully held at Singapore EXPO. Co-organized by Tencent Cloud Security and A\*STAR (Agency for Science, Technology and Research), among others, this workshop brought together world-leading researchers to explore the trustworthiness and controllability of intelligent agents, laying a solid safety foundation for Agentic AI.

---

## Background: From Conversational AI to Action-Based AI — A Paradigm Shift

人工智能正经历一场深刻变革：从被动响应的对话系统，转向能自主规划、调用工具、持续协作的**行为式智能体（Agentic AI）**。

> AI is undergoing a profound shift — from passively responding conversational systems to Agentic AI that autonomously plans, invokes tools, and collaborates continuously.

这种能力在医疗、教育、机器人、企业自动化等领域展现出巨大潜力，却也带来了前所未有的安全挑战：

> While this unlocks enormous potential in healthcare, education, robotics, and enterprise automation, it also introduces unprecedented safety challenges:

- 如何确保智能体与人类价值观对齐？ &nbsp; `How to ensure agents align with human values?`
- 如何在复杂现实中保持稳健？ &nbsp; `How to remain robust in complex real-world settings?`
- 如何验证其行为以建立持久信任？ &nbsp; `How to verify agent behavior to build lasting trust?`

---

## Workshop Focus: Alignment · Robustness · Verifiability

我们汇聚了来自全球学术界、产业界与政策制定领域的领先声音，共同探讨了智能体的可信构建、安全对齐、人格建模、评估基准与治理框架等核心前沿议题。

> The workshop convened leading voices from academia, industry, and policy-making worldwide to discuss core frontier topics including trustworthy agent construction, safety alignment, personality modeling, evaluation benchmarks, and governance frameworks.

## Tencent Cloud Security: Building a Solid Safety Foundation for Agentic AI

作为云与AI安全研究的先行者，腾讯云安全致力于将产业一线的安全实践融入学术前沿，推动智能体技术安全可控落地。

> As a pioneer in cloud and AI security research, Tencent Cloud Security is dedicated to integrating real-world security practices into cutting-edge academia, driving safe and controllable deployment of agent technologies.

我们始终相信，Agentic AI 的能力需要与安全的根基同步建设。这源于我们持续的产业实践：

> We firmly believe that the power of Agentic AI must be built in lockstep with a solid safety foundation. This conviction stems from our sustained industry practice:

- **以实战验证边界** / **Validate Boundaries Through Real-World Attacks**

  两个月前，我们举办的"腾讯云黑客松·智能渗透挑战赛"首次大规模模拟AI智能体驱动的攻防对抗，验证了智能体在复杂安全场景中的行为边界与风险点，为可信AI研究提供了鲜活样本。

  > Two months ago, our "Tencent Cloud Hackathon · Agentic Penetration Challenge" conducted the first large-scale simulation of AI-agent-driven offensive and defensive engagements, validating agent behavior boundaries and risk points in complex security scenarios — providing real-world samples for trustworthy AI research.

- **以体系构建信任** / **Build Trust Through Systematic Discovery**

  通过腾讯安全众测平台，我们持续构建开放协同的安全发现机制，这与 Agentic AI 的"可验证性"需求高度共鸣。

  > Through the Tencent Security Crowdsourcing Platform, we continuously build an open and collaborative security discovery mechanism — resonating deeply with the "verifiability" imperative of Agentic AI.

- **以原生能力护航** / **Safeguard with Cloud-Native Capabilities**

  腾讯云安全长期积累的云原生安全能力，涵盖威胁检测、数据保护与系统韧性，已自然延伸至AI智能体的架构安全、行为监控与风险干预，为智能体的自主行动提供全程护航。

  > Tencent Cloud Security's long-standing cloud-native capabilities — spanning threat detection, data protection, and system resilience — have naturally extended to agent architecture security, behavioral monitoring, and risk intervention, providing end-to-end protection for autonomous agent actions.
